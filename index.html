Connect this page to your Google Sheet

The board reads and writes a Google Sheet through a small Apps Script web app that you own. Nothing is stored on any third-party server. Paste the two values from your deployment:

  1. The web app URL from Apps Script (Deploy → Manage deployments, ends in /exec).
  2. The token from the sheet's config!B1 cell.

Two people write to this board: you and Claude

The data lives in a Google Sheet you own (Outreach Desk DB), not in the page. This page talks to it through an Apps Script web app in your own Google account; Claude reads and writes the same sheet from the chat through the Google Sheets connector. The page re-reads the sheet every 20 seconds, so a change made in the chat, in the sheet itself, or on another device shows up here within half a minute. You can also open the sheet and edit any cell directly.

The one thing Claude cannot do

Claude is blocked from typing into LinkedIn's message and invitation boxes — that block sits above your approval and cannot be waived from the chat. So the division of labour is fixed: Claude drafts and tracks, you send. Every card carries a finished message, a copy button and a link straight to the profile, so sending is three clicks and no typing.

What to say to Claude

Plain sentences work. These all land as writes to this board:

Who is due today? Mahnas accepted — log it and draft the opener. Nobody replied to the five I sent Tuesday. Write follow-up 1 for all of them. Add 15 finance directors in Saudi on the ZATCA play, with notes. Soji booked a call for Thursday 4pm. Update him and prep me. Park anyone who has been silent for three weeks.

The lifecycle

How the follow-up clock is set

Every status change stamps a date on the record and schedules the next one automatically, so nothing needs a reminder set by hand. Change a status and the lead disappears from Due now until its next date arrives. If a date is wrong, open the lead and edit it — the board takes your date over its own rule.
